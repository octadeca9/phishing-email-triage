SOC Analyst Working Notes — Case PHISH-2026-0042
=================================================

Synthetic training case. All domains use .test/.invalid, all IPs are from the
RFC 5737 documentation range, and every URL is defanged. Nothing here is live:
do not attempt to visit URLs or open attachments.

Observed so far
---------------
- Gateway alert raised: "Potential Sender Impersonation" (severity: medium).
- Sender display name claims "Finance Operations" but the From domain is not an
  organization or trusted partner domain (see reference/trusted_domains.json).
- Authentication results are mixed: SPF pass, but DKIM none and DMARC fail.
  Reminder: SPF pass alone does not make an email safe — check alignment.
- Reply-To points to a different domain than From.
- Body contains a single defanged URL leading to a generic "review" path.
- One attachment is declared as PDF but its observed container type differs.
- Two recipients (finance01, finance02). Delivery, open, and click activity is
  captured across gateway / mailbox / proxy event logs.

Open questions to resolve from the evidence
--------------------------------------------
- Which recipients opened the message, and did anyone click the link?
- Was the click successful or blocked? Any sign of credential submission?
- What is the proportionate verdict, severity, and containment response?
